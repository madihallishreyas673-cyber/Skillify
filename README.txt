OPPORTUNITY ENGINE - make your Android APK (no coding)

Files in this folder: index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png

STEP 1 - Put the app online (free, 2 minutes)
 1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser (sign up free).
 2. Drag the whole folder (all 5 files) onto the page.
 3. Copy the https:// link it gives you. Open it on your phone to test.

STEP 2 - Turn the link into an APK (free)
 1. Open https://www.pwabuilder.com
 2. Paste your link, tap Start.
 3. Tap "Package for stores" > Android > Generate.
 4. Download the zip. Inside is the .apk (to install) and a signing key.
 5. KEEP the signing key file and password safe. You need them for updates.

STEP 3 - Install
 Send the .apk to your phone and open it. Allow "install unknown apps" if asked.
